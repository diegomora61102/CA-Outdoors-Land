        Land, campground, trail and lake data comes from the BLM, USDA Forest Service and US Census and is refreshed monthly. Forest Service open/closed status is checked daily, and temporary closures, active wildfires (NIFC) and National Weather Service alerts are checked every hour. Weather updates hourly from NWS stations and forecasts: a station reading where one is close by and at a similar elevation, otherwise an estimate from nearby stations corrected for elevation. BLM doesn't publish open/closed status, and dispersed camping is allowed on most BLM land unless posted. Ownership is approximate at parcel edges; check with the managing office before you rely on it on the ground. Basemaps from the USGS National Map.
